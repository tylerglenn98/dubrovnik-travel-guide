Travel Guide

Plan Your Dubrovnik Visit

Simple advice for transportation, safety, food, packing, and enjoying the city with confidence.

Before You Go

Getting Around

Old Town is best explored on foot. Comfortable shoes are important because the stone streets can be uneven and many areas include stairs.

Solo Safety

I felt safe during my visit, but travelers should remain aware of their surroundings, protect valuables, and plan transportation in advance.

Food to Try

Look for seafood, pasta, pastries, coffee, and gelato throughout the historic center and waterfront areas.

What to Pack

Bring comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a reusable water bottle, a portable charger, and a light layer.

Dining in Dubrovnik

One of the best parts of traveling is slowing down and enjoying a good meal. Dubrovnik offers seafood, pasta, pastries, cafés, and desserts throughout Old Town and near the waterfront.

From My Experience

Make Time to Sit and Enjoy the City

My trip was not only about sightseeing. Taking time to enjoy a meal, people-watch, and appreciate the surroundings made the experience feel more relaxed and memorable.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Begin with an early walk through Old Town.
  2. Visit the city walls or a historic fort.
  3. Stop for lunch, coffee, or gelato.
  4. Spend the afternoon near the harbor or waterfront.
  5. Finish with dinner and an evening view of the city.
