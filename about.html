Meet Tyler Glenn

The Traveler Behind the Website

My name is Tyler Glenn, and I am a student studying cybersecurity and web development. I enjoy technology, travel, photography, trying new restaurants, and learning skills that allow me to create useful digital projects. This website gave me the opportunity to combine web design with a travel experience that was meaningful to me.

Dubrovnik became special to me because it was the destination of my first international solo trip as a 28-year-old woman. Traveling alone helped me become more confident and independent. I felt safe in Dubrovnik, the people were kind, and the scenery was beautiful. I selected this destination because I wanted to highlight a European city that is not discussed as often as places such as Paris or Rome.

This website is intended to help visitors learn about Dubrovnik's history, attractions, and practical travel information. I also wanted it to show that solo international travel can be exciting and rewarding when it is planned carefully.
